FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you create custom itineraries?

Yes — every trip is planned around your dates, budget and interests rather than a fixed package.

Can I request Kerala, India and international trips?

Yes, we plan journeys across Kerala, the rest of India and international destinations.

Can I change my itinerary after it's planned?

Yes, itineraries can be adjusted as your dates or preferences change, subject to availability.

Is there a minimum group size?

No — we plan trips for solo travellers, couples, families and groups alike.

How do I get a price for my trip?

Share your destination, dates and traveller count via the planner form, WhatsApp or a call, and we'll get back to you with a quote.

What's included in a planned trip?

Stays, transport and activities are laid out once your itinerary is planned with you — nothing is added without your confirmation.

How do I reach My Ashtamudi directly?

Call or WhatsApp us at +919645932230, or visit us in Thekkumbhagam, Kerala.
